# Persistence for Ten-Year-Olds

A deliberately flat pedagogical working repository for teaching the structure of **Persistence / the Constraint Grammar of Completed Intelligibility** to children.

The aim is not to simplify away the system. It is to give children a route into the same 6 × 3 × 3 structure through ordinary language, comparison, sorting, discussion, and recognition.

## Files

### `goldmine.md`

The generation instrument.

It gives an independent GPT the primitive grammar and asks for ten ordinary companion utterances for each of the 54 coordinates.

Its purpose is to expose each cell's natural utterance field: convergence, variation, recurring attractors, and possible instability.

Generated utterances are evidence and pedagogical material, **not automatically canonical answers**.

### `pixels_of_clarity.md`

The cleaned pedagogical corpus.

It merges three independent Goldmine runs:

- 1,620 raw utterances;
- 1,307 retained after conservative duplicate / near-duplicate consolidation;
- 313 collapsed as duplicates or close paraphrases.

Original retained wording is preserved. Structural disagreements are flagged rather than silently corrected.

This is the main source material from which the ten-year-old teaching system can now be built.

## Working pedagogical direction

The emerging route is:

**experience → comparison → discrimination → recognition → naming → map → transfer**

Children should first encounter structures in ordinary sentences, learn to compare and sort them, and only then acquire the formal names and the complete 54-position map.

The intended destination is a child who can use three structural questions:

1. **What kind of change happened?**
2. **What made it count as complete?**
3. **How does the achieved result remain available?**

Those questions lead into:

- 6 Transformation Patterns;
- 3 Completion Topologies;
- 3 Persistence Modes;
- 54 coordinates.

The search procedure may be taught sequentially for navigation, while the underlying cell remains a simultaneous conjunction of all three dimensions.

## Principle

**Do not explain before there is something to notice.**

The goal is a visceral grasp of the structure, not memorization of a taxonomy.
