    <div class="section-heading"><div><p class="eyebrow">About the project</p><h2 id="about-title">The map is not the lesson.</h2></div><p>The achievement is learning to notice recurrent structure, compare neighbours, tolerate ambiguity, ask what the evidence permits, and test the map rather than worship it.
